Refund Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

We want you to be happy with every paid course you buy from PGC Educator. This policy explains when you can get your money back and how to ask. It applies to paid online courses and paid notes. Our free video lessons on pgceducator.in need no payment, so no refund applies to them.

In short

  • You can ask for a refund within 7 calendar days of buying a paid online course.
  • Refund requests go to info.pgceducator@gmail.com.
  • We review your request in 2 to 3 business days, and the money reaches your bank or UPI in 5 to 7 business days after that.

1. 7-day refund for paid online courses

You can get a refund on a paid online course if both of these are true:

  • You ask within 7 calendar days of the purchase, and
  • You have accessed less than 20% of the course modules or videos, and you have not downloaded the bulk PDF notes in full.

2. What is not refundable

  • Standalone downloadable digital PDFs and MCQ sheets, once you have accessed them.
  • Requests made after the 7-day window.

3. Duplicate purchases

If you paid for the same item twice by mistake, tell us within 48 hours and we will refund the duplicate payment in full.

4. How to ask for a refund

Send an email to info.pgceducator@gmail.com with the following details:

  • Your name
  • Your registered email address or phone number
  • The name of the course you bought
  • The transaction ID of your payment
  • The reason for your request

5. How long it takes

  • Review: we review your request and start the refund within 2 to 3 business days.
  • Credit: once we start the refund, the money reaches your bank account or UPI in 5 to 7 business days, depending on your bank.

The refund is sent back to the payment method you used for the purchase.

6. Contact us

If you have a question about this policy, write to info.pgceducator@gmail.com. Please also read our Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.
