Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

PGC Educator (pgceducator.in) is an educational website founded by Priyanka Gusain Chandra in Delhi, India. We share free NCERT Social Science video lessons for Classes 6 to 10, chapter explanations, MCQs and General Knowledge topics. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it and what choices you have. By using the website you agree to this policy.

1. Information you give us

You never need to give us any information to watch the free lessons. We only receive details that you choose to share:

  • Contact form. When you fill in our Google Form, we receive what you type, such as your name, class, contact details and your question.
  • Email. If you write to info.pgceducator@gmail.com, we receive your email address and your message.
  • Student Login (optional). If you use the Student Login box, we receive the name, class and email or WhatsApp number you enter. These details are stored in a Google Sheet managed by us. Every field is optional and you can skip the box completely.
  • Your browser. If you enter a name or skip the Student Login box, your browser remembers this (using local storage) so that the box does not appear again and the button can say “Hi, your name”. This stays on your own device.

2. Information collected automatically

When you visit the website, some technical and usage data is collected by our website host and by the tools we use to understand how the website is used:

  • Website hosting (GitHub Pages). Our host may log technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request, to deliver and secure the website.
  • Google Analytics through Google Tag Manager. We use these tools to see which pages and lessons are visited, how visitors reach the website, the type of device and browser used, and your approximate location (such as city). This helps us improve our lessons.
  • Meta Pixel. We may use the Meta Pixel to understand how visitors reach and use the website and how our posts perform on Facebook and Instagram.

This data is used as general statistics. We do not use it to identify individual students.

3. How we use your information

  • To reply to your questions and requests, including help with notes and lessons.
  • To improve our lessons, topics and the website.
  • To keep the website working and secure.
  • To send you updates, only if you have asked for them.
  • To follow the law when required.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel may use cookies or similar technologies to recognise a browser and measure visits. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and the website still works without them. You can also use the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

5. Third-party services and links

Our website uses and links to services run by other companies. They collect data under their own privacy policies, and we do not control how they do this:

  • YouTube. Video lessons play on the website in an embedded YouTube player. YouTube and Google may collect data about your viewing. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Forms and Google Sheets. Our contact form and the Student Login details are handled by Google, under the same Google Privacy Policy.
  • Instagram and Facebook. These are run by Meta. See the Meta Privacy Policy.
  • Telegram. If you join our Telegram channel, Telegram’s own policy applies. See the Telegram Privacy Policy.

6. Children’s privacy

Our lessons are made for students in Classes 6 to 10, and many of our visitors are under 18 years of age. We take this seriously:

  • Students can watch every free lesson without sharing any personal details.
  • The contact form and the Student Login box are optional. Students under 18 should ask a parent or guardian before sharing their name, class or contact details.
  • We ask only for the details we need and we never sell them.
  • A parent or guardian can write to us at info.pgceducator@gmail.com to ask what we hold about their child, or to ask us to correct or delete it.

7. Sharing and data security

We do not sell or rent your personal data to anyone. We share it only with the service providers needed to run the website (such as Google and our website host), or when the law requires us to.

We use reasonable steps to protect the information we hold. No website or online storage is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

8. How long we keep data and your choices

We keep the details you send us only for as long as we need them to reply to you, to help you and to keep our records. You can email us at any time to:

  • ask what details we hold about you,
  • correct them,
  • ask us to delete them, or
  • withdraw your permission for us to use them.

We will act on your request as soon as we reasonably can.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed. If you keep using the website after a change, you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact us

For any question about this policy or your data, write to info.pgceducator@gmail.com. You can also use the contact form on our home page.
