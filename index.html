Welcome to PGC Educator. Here I share Social Studies learning resources for Classes 6 to 10, mainly based on NCERT, explained in a simple and easy-to-understand way. My aim is to help students understand concepts clearly and build a strong foundation in Social Studies, rather than simply memorising the content.

Along with the regular school syllabus, I also bring interesting and informative topics that help students explore new ideas, improve their general knowledge and learn beyond the classroom.

Whether you are studying for school, revising important concepts, or simply curious to learn something new, PGC Educator is here to make your learning journey easier and more interesting.
